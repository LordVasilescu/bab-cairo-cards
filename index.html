You start at zero. Hear a word, see how it sounds in English letters, use it once with the answer showing, then we hide it and ask. The cafe is the classroom. The cards are the words.

Whose mouth are you using?

Egyptian conjugates for the speaker. This picks كويس vs كويسة, عايز vs عايزة.

How this actually teaches you

  1. Hear it. The game says the word out loud. You don’t need the alphabet yet.
  2. See the sound. Aywa is English letters for أيوه. That’s your on-ramp.
  3. The Arabic sits there anyway so your eyes get used to the shapes.
  4. First use is open-book. The meaning is on the card. Play it. That’s practice, not a test.
  5. Next time we hide it and ask. Miss it and you take a point of damage — the word stays in your hand.

First cafe: six words. Hi, yes, no, how are you, I’m good, thanks. That’s a real conversation.
